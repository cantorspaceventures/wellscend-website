# Wellscend Design System

**Vibe**: Calm, Confident, Human, Visual, Aesthetically Beautiful.
**Palette**: Earthy, grounded, yet diverse.

## Color Palette (From User)
- **Primary Background (Warm White)**: `#F6F5F0`
- **Primary Text (Deep Earth Brown)**: `#3E342A`
- **Secondary Text (Warm Umber)**: `#6B5A48`
- **Accent 1 (Wellscend Sage)**: `#B6B08A`
- **Accent 2 (Soft Mineral Blue)**: `#6F86A6`
- **Neutral (Warm Stone Grey)**: `#9C9A93`

## Typography
- **Headings**: 'Outfit', sans-serif (Modern, friendly, clean)
- **Body**: 'Inter', sans-serif (Legible, neutral)

## Visual Direction (Stitch-Enhanced)
- **Hero**: Use a full-width or split layout with a stunning abstract visual representing "understanding" and "flow".
- **Imagery**: Soft, organic shapes. Avoid stock photos of people in yoga poses. Use abstract, textural elements that evoke calmness.
- **Glassmorphism**: Use subtle transparency with `#F6F5F0` to keep it grounded but modern.
- **Micro-interactions**: Hover states on cards should lift and subtly shift color.

## Components
- **Buttons**: Rounded (Pill), Sage or Deep Brown background.
- **Cards**: Warm White or very light transparency, soft shadows.
